# Global E-commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis
## Insights Summary

### Project Objective
Analyze global e-commerce transactions from 2023–2025 to identify key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, profitability issues, and operational opportunities.

---

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains:

- 2,000 orders
- 1,534 unique customers
- 20 countries
- 40 products
- Approximately $500M total revenue
- Approximately $160M total profit

The data spans from January 2023 to December 2025.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Revenue and Growth Trends

Business performance fluctuated over the three-year period.

- Revenue and order volume declined from 2023 to 2024 but recovered in 2025.
- Monthly sales patterns suggest recurring demand cycles approximately every 2–4 months.
- No strong long-term seasonality pattern was observed.

Insight:
Business demand appears cyclical rather than steadily increasing.

Recommendation:
Further analysis should investigate external factors such as promotions, campaigns, or regional events driving recurring spikes.

---

### 2. Regional Performance

North America, Asia Pacific, and Europe generated the highest revenue.

However:

- Asia Pacific had high order volume but relatively low profit margins.
- South America and Middle East & Africa showed high shipping costs.
- South America recorded the highest shipping cost relative to sales (29.62%).

Insight:
Strong sales volume does not necessarily translate into strong profitability.

Recommendation:
Investigate logistics efficiency and pricing strategies in underperforming regions.

---

### 3. Customer Segment Analysis

Consumer customers generated:

- Highest order volume
- Highest revenue
- Highest profit contribution

Insight:
Consumer customers represent the company's most valuable segment.

Recommendation:
Allocate greater marketing resources and retention efforts toward consumer customers.

---

### 4. Product Category Performance

Furniture generated the highest overall profit, followed by Technology and Clothing & Accessories.

Office Supplies showed weaker performance overall.

Insight:
Product categories contribute unevenly to profitability.

Recommendation:
Increase investment in high-performing categories while reviewing lower-performing categories.

---

### 5. Product-Level Analysis

Top performers:

- Ergonomic Office Chair
- Standing Desk Converter

High-margin opportunity:

- Business Casual Blazer (36.24% profit margin)

Underperformers:

- Paper Clips Box 500pc
- Highlighters Neon Pack 6
- Sticky Notes Multicolor 6-Pack

Insight:
Several low-performing products are concentrated within Office Supplies.

Recommendation:
Review pricing, promotion, and inventory strategy for weak products and consider discontinuation if losses persist.

---

### 6. Discount Strategy Analysis

Findings show:

- Larger discounts reduced profit margins.
- Low discount groups achieved the highest orders, revenue, and profit.

Insight:
Aggressive discounting increases sales volume but damages profitability.

Recommendation:
Optimize discount strategy and prioritize lower discount levels.

---

### 7. Payment Behavior

Credit Card and PayPal were the most frequently used payment methods.

Credit Card customers generated:

- Highest order count
- Highest revenue
- Highest profit

Insight:
Payment method usage appears associated with customer spending behavior.

Recommendation:
Encourage preferred payment methods through targeted incentives.

---

### 8. Shipping Cost Impact

Regions with high shipping costs generally experienced lower profitability.

Most affected:

- South America
- Middle East & Africa

Insight:
Shipping expenses significantly impact margins.

Recommendation:
Explore logistics optimization, alternative fulfillment strategies, or regional partnerships.

---

## Overall Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize Consumer customer acquisition and retention.
2. Expand investment in profitable products and categories.
3. Review low-performing Office Supply products.
4. Optimize discount strategy to protect margins.
5. Improve logistics efficiency in high-cost regions.
6. Investigate low profitability in Asia Pacific.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis revealed that revenue alone does not determine business success. Profitability is strongly influenced by customer mix, discount strategies, product performance, and shipping costs. Improving operational efficiency and focusing on high-value segments can create stronger long-term business performance.
